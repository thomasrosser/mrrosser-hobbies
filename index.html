A Little Bit About Me

Hello! My name is Mr. Rosser. I am from Wales and 39 years old.

For the past 17 years, I have lived and worked abroad as a teacher, gaining valuable experience in many fascinating countries. My teaching journey has taken me to:

  • Qatar
  • Vietnam
  • China
  • Egypt
  • Oman
  • Bahrain

My Favourite Hobbies

Golf

Golf is a great passion of mine. I enjoy the challenge and tranquility of the game, whether I'm playing 18 holes or just practicing on the driving range. It's a fantastic way to relax and meet new people.

Football (Soccer)

I am a massive football fan. My favourite teams are **Manchester United** (Man Utd) and the mighty **Wrexham AFC**. I enjoy watching matches and keeping up with the latest news and results from both the Premier League and lower divisions.

Rugby

As a Welshman, **rugby** holds a special place in my heart. I avidly follow the **Welsh national rugby team** in all their international campaigns, particularly during the Six Nations Championship. Nothing beats the atmosphere of a great rugby match!

Travelling

After living abroad for so long, it's no surprise that travelling is one of my top hobbies. I love exploring new cultures, trying local food, and seeing historical sites. Each new country is an adventure waiting to happen!
